Coffee Extraction Technology

Pressure and Vacuum Systems for Cold Coffee

VacPress uses piston movement to create positive and negative pressure inside the cup. This pressure action enhances coffee extraction in cold water.

VacBrew systems use vacuum force to pull icy cold water through coffee grounds, producing fresh and concentrated coffee while helping preserve flavor and aroma.
