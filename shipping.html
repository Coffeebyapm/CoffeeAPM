Shipping Information

Thank you for shopping with Coffee APM. We currently plan to serve customers within the United States.

1. Shipping Charges

Product prices do not include shipping fees. Shipping is charged separately based on package weight, dimensions, delivery destination, and available shipping services.

Customers must receive the complete product and shipping total before making payment. If shipping is not shown at checkout, please contact us for a quotation before completing your purchase.

2. Shipping Within the United States

Coffee APM plans to use USPS initially for eligible smaller products.

Shipping charges depend on the package and delivery address. Available services may vary by product size and destination.

3. Brown Rice Snack Shipping

To help customers save on shipping, Coffee APM offers flat Standard shipping fees based on the number of Brown Rice Snack bags ordered.

Order Quantity Standard Shipping Fee
1–5 bags $15.00 per order
6–9 bags $20.00 per order
10–50 bags (up to one full box) $25.00 per order

Save on Shipping! The same Standard shipping fee applies throughout each quantity range. Ordering more bags together can reduce the shipping cost per bag.

Express Shipping

Express shipping is available upon request. The estimated minimum Express shipping charge is $37.05 for eligible packages weighing up to 5 lbs.

Actual Express shipping costs depend on package weight, dimensions, and destination. Please contact Coffee APM for a quotation before ordering Express delivery.

Published flat rates are intended for eligible U.S. destinations. Shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories requires confirmation before payment. Orders exceeding 50 bags require a separate shipping quotation.

4. Alaska and Hawaii Shipping

We welcome inquiries from customers in Alaska and Hawaii.

Shipping costs may differ from those in the 48 contiguous states. The applicable shipping amount will be confirmed before payment.

5. VacBrew Commercial Equipment

VacBrew Commercial Cold Brew Extractors require an individual shipping quotation.

Please contact Coffee APM with your delivery destination and the equipment you wish to purchase. We will discuss shipping arrangements and total costs before payment.

Optional Setup and Training Service

Setup and training services are available upon customer request for an additional fee.

These services are optional and are not included in the standard product price. Please contact Coffee APM for service availability, details, and a quotation.

This option applies to all VacBrew Commercial Cold Brew Extractor models, including the 7-Gallon, 15-Gallon, 20-Gallon, and 50-Gallon models.

Request Commercial Shipping / Setup Quote

6. International Shipping

International shipping is not available at this time.

Orders to U.S. territories require shipping confirmation before payment.
