How to Purchase Our Products

Thank you for your interest in Coffee APM products. We offer secure payment options through Stripe and PayPal.

1. Select Your Product

Visit our homepage and browse our product descriptions, photographs, and videos.

View Coffee APM Products

2. Choose Your Payment Method

Eligible products offer Stripe credit/debit card payments and PayPal payments.

Select your preferred payment method on the product listing. Please confirm that the checkout shows the correct product and applicable shipping charges before paying.

3. Shipping Charges

Product prices do not include shipping. Shipping charges are paid separately from the listed product price.

We initially serve customers within the United States. USPS shipping charges depend on package weight, dimensions, and destination. Alaska and Hawaii shipping costs may differ from those in the contiguous United States.

If checkout does not display the complete product and shipping total, please contact Coffee APM for a shipping quotation before making payment.
